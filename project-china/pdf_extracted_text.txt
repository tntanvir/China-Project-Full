ABOUT US
Wuping Feitian Electronics Technology Co., Ltd. is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in printed circuit boards 
(PCBs), with a registered capital of 30 million RMB. The company owns three factory buildings with a production area 
of ​​15,000 square meters, located at No. 4, 5, and 6 Xingfu 2nd Road, Yanqian Industrial Park, Wuping High-tech Zone, 
Fujian Province. The factory is bordered by Shanghang County, Fujian Province to the east, Jiangxi Province to the west, 
Guangdong Province to the south, and Changting County, Fujian Province to the north. It is a crucial transportation hub and 
distribution center for goods in western Fujian, eastern Guangdong, and southern Jiangxi, known as the "Golden Triangle" of 
western Fujian. The company boasts a complete supply chain, with daily logistics and express delivery services to and from 
all over the country, ensuring convenient transportation and rapid service. It has a stable management team and over 300 
skilled technical employees, equipped with advanced production and testing equipment in the PCB industry. Furthermore, it 
has established and obtained certifications for modern enterprise management systems, including National Military Standard, 
ISO9000, ISO/T/S16949, UL, CQC, ISO14001, and CQCThe company's printed circuit boards are mainly sold to large 
domestic electronics enterprises in the Pearl River Delta and Yangtze River Delta regions, as well as to Europe, America, 
Japan, South Korea, and Southeast Asia. The company's monthly production area reaches 100000 square meters, with up 
to 1,200 different models produced monthly.With increasingly fierce market competition, Feitian Company has placed stricter  
standards on quality, efficiency, and cost, fully leveraging its advantages in market, technology, and talent to strive to provide 
customers with the highest quality and fastest products and services.

Team/Technical Advantages
The company was founded by a professor from Shenzhen University. Chairman 
Chen Jingqiu is a professor and Doctor of Engineering at Shenzhen University. 
Leveraging the resources of Shenzhen University, he has long been engaged in 
forward-looking research on the development of the PCB industry, conducting 
systematic research on key areas of the PCB sector. This allows the technical 
team to stay abreast of the latest cutting-edge technologies in the PCB industry. 
The company already possesses numerous independent 5G invention patents, 
making it the company with the most authorized 5G-related invention patents 
in Longyan City, and the only company with the capability to R&D and produce 
5G high-frequency boards.
